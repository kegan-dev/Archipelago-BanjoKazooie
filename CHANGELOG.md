# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (alpha)

The first public release, for testing.

- The whole game as Archipelago checks: 100 jiggies, 115 Mumbo tokens, 20 molehills, 24 empty honeycombs, 45 Jinjos,
  the 8 note doors' counts and, with `notesanity`, all 900 notes.
- Logic from HarbourMasters' Lighthouse randomizer, with three difficulties (`logic_type`).
- Bottles' moves, honeycombs and Jinjos shuffled or left in place; Spiral Mountain's nine moves to start with.
- The Lair's nine world doors shuffled (`randomize_world_entrances`), and quick transform with D-pad left.
- Death link, tag link, five traps, and egg and feather refills as filler.
- Notes stay collected, lives are infinite, and the intro and the Lair's first cutscene can be skipped.
- The client makes the ROM from your own Banjo-Kazooie (USA) v1.0 ROM.
- Played on Project64 3.0.1 and ares v148.
